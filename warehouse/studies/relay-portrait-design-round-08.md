# Relay portrait design lab — Round 08
Status: TRAINING ONLY; visual quality gate NOT PASSED.

## Problem inherited
The prior Relay portrait looked crude despite technical PNG correctness. Round 07's broken halo crowded the hood and the overall design remained generic and too rounded. Canonical Relay needs a distinctive CRT-like face, asymmetrical weathered hood, broken antenna/relay ring, layered fabric and meaningful material depth; old Echo Frames are damaged and glitchy but those are secondary to good character design.

## Exercise and evidence
- Read Atlas Warehouse INDEX and both the previous failure critique and Round 06 material notes before starting.
- Reviewed Saint11's cluster-sketching, Rivals Workshop's simplification, and a silhouette black-fill tutorial.
- Authored three original 104x128 RGBA silhouette thumbnails by integer-pixel raster editing with Pillow. Body held constant; fragmented halo placement varied. No AI-generated/converted images.
- Measured separate ring/body masks: A minimum gap 2.83 px, B 3.61 px, C 4.00 px; no direct pixel overlap in any of the three. **This is geometry only, not a visual-quality rating.**
- B has the clearest left displaced relay ring; C loses the strong left rim and becomes top-heavy. A appears crowded above the hood. Selected B ONLY as a candidate for another exercise.
- Separately practiced a 112x100 3/4 angled CRT module: an uneven bezel, layered bone panels, receding screen glass, and functional side-head display. No overgrowth, glitch animation, or texture overlays were added because base construction is not approved.
- Exported transparent PNGs plus review board with 1x previews on both pale and dark backgrounds. Editable raster source and QA report exist in the study conversation: relay_round08_silhouette_lab.py and relay_round08_QA.json.

## Honest critique
All silhouettes still look too much like rounded generic heads and the shoulders lack character-specific shape language. B's clearance is better than A's, but none meets the canonical Relay design standard. The CRT perspective exercise remains simplified and its material transitions need hand-refined contour and depth work. Do not promote this work into game assets.

## Next correction
Keep B's broken offset ring; redraw HOOD and SHOULDERS alone into three sharper asymmetrical alternatives. Judge the silhouettes at native size before adding material detail. After a viable silhouette exists, refine the CRT's near/far bezel thickness, large clusters and lighting hierarchy. Then and only then add age and malfunction effects. Maintain all other pixel-art training tasks in parallel.
