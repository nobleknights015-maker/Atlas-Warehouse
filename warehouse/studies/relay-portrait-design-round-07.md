# Relay portrait design lab — Round 07
Status: PRACTICE / artistic quality gate FAILED (not production-approved).

## Starting failure
The Founder assessed the Round 06 Relay portrait as looking like a basic MS Paint sketch. This is an art/design failure, not a PNG validation failure. The previous version overused round or symmetric shapes, shallow shoulder planes and flat colored regions. Extra moss or glitch effects did not correct its construction.

## Canonical design target
Relay is an old Ghost Grid-era Echo Frame, not a generic clean robot. The reference has a tilted CRT-like face, asymmetrical torn off-white hood, deep layered scarf, broken relay halo, distinct shoulder armor, exposed machinery, archived data tags, and age/overgrowth. Recognizability must survive even with effects turned off.

## Applied practice
Hand-authored 208x208 RGBA pixel painting via integer-coordinate raster edits in Python/Pillow (source held in study conversation). No image generation, photographic resizing or automatic pixelization. Three passes: silhouette; CRT perspective/armor/light planes; limited age/vegetation. Two alternate silhouette compositions A/B adjust separation between the broken halo and hood.

Technical validations for the native PNG: alpha values only 0 and 255, 29 opaque colors, independent silhouette pass, no animation. Technical checks do not imply professional visual quality.

## What changed
The CRT frame is no longer circular: glass, angled bezel and jaw are separated and turned. Hood, scarf, armor and display are staged in depth. Bone white / plum / graphite / teal provide material contrast. Variant B opens a clearer negative-space gap on the left side of the halo.

## Critical review / failure retained
At true 1x, the top arc still merges with the hood in the silhouette and the overall mass remains overly broad. Tear patterns are too uniform, shoulders need much stronger structure and the image has not yet attained the angular sophistication and reference fidelity of the canonical character. Variant B is a partial correction, not a passing result. Increasing canvas size from 144x144 to 208x208 does not in itself make the artwork better.

## Next exercise
Generate three extremely small monochrome thumbnail alternatives with different halo placements and hood directions, select the strongest at 1x, then study ONLY CRT 3/4 perspective and layered hood armor with hand-tuned pixel-step contour rhythm. Reject an unconvincing silhouette before shading, growth or animation. Continue other pixel-art, gait, overgrowth, and malfunction studies separately.
