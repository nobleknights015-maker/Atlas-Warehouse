# Starter Sleeper Frame Unit 09 — first static sprite study
Status: REFERENCE-DERIVED TRAINING ONLY. NOT ORIGINAL DRAWING, NOT ENGINE-READY.

## Why switch subjects
The Founder supplied a clear Unit 09 starter Sleeper Frame design and existing high-quality pixel-style sheets. Previous practice portraits of Relay were too complicated and too crudely constructed. The immediate goal is to learn one static reference before more animation, overgrowth and glitch effects.

## Locked visual identity
- Asymmetric overlapping bone-white helmet plates, not hair
- Black visor with narrow violet glowing line
- Light off-white shoulder/chest/leg armor over dark articulated understructure
- Marked 09 shoulder, exposed red cabling and subtle violet leg emissives
- Lean humanoid proportions and differentiated knees, ankles, hands and feet
Do not invent bear ears or remove mandatory side-head/chest technology features when relevant.

## Actual exercise, Nov 2026
Examined the supplied reference sheet, selected the first front-standing pose, and isolated its largest alpha-connected component without altering any surviving pixels. On the source sheet (1448x1086 RGBA), the standing figure occupied x=161..285, y=13..333 (125x321 pixels) and was placed unchanged onto a transparent 160x352 canvas. The source retains many translucent edge pixels, so it should NOT be mislabeled as crisp limited-palette original pixel art.

Created a comparative non-dither color reduction with 32/64/96 color trials; generic quantization visibly washed out character-defining violet optics. Reserved a violet emissive palette while reducing the other materials. A vivid first reserve pass oversaturated the face; a second pass used a restrained source-near hue/value ramp. The refined comparison has 64 RGB colors among significantly opaque pixels and maintains the standing silhouette, 09 shoulder mark and violet highlights more faithfully.

This is a source-based reference study and palette experiment. No fresh sprite or original drawing was created, and no independently animated frames were produced. Technical format and color-count checks are NOT a pass on character-design skill.

## Core reusable findings
1. When good reference sprite art already exists, isolate and lock it as an objective quality baseline rather than replacing it with crude programmatic shapes.
2. Preserve silhouette, limb proportions, shoulder markings, panel geometry, visor hue/value and lights before any style conversion.
3. Global palette reduction can erase tiny emissive regions; assign a separate small violet ramp and compare in context at 1x on dark/light backgrounds.
4. A bright protected glow can look worse than the source; evaluate value and saturation, not just whether the bright hue survives.
5. Do not call an extracted/quantized sprite new hand-painted art. The NEXT independent training exercise must demonstrate actual drawing from a blank canvas.
