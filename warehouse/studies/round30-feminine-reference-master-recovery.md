## Lessons

Human-like flow comes from purposeful weight, anticipation, overlapping timings and settling while mechanical segment lengths stay fixed. Blend elbow bend poles through crossed-arm entry instead of flipping branch. An open gesture needs open-hand source material. A near temple hand needs deliberate foreground depth ordering. Fold seated legs with constrained fixed-length FK; do not let a free knee pole change sides. Lift the hip path during the prone transition to preserve knee/floor clearance. Separate feet that deliberately unweight from planted support assertions. Preserve exact frame boundaries for authored action chains. Store failures separately so future sessions do not repeat them.
